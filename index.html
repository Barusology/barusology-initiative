FAQ

Before you ask

How much does a project cost?
Small automation builds start lowest; a full two-store app with a backend is the larger end. Either way you get a fixed written price after the scope call, so there is no meter running.
How long until something is live?
A clickable prototype inside the first week. A public launch is usually four to eight weeks after that, depending on how quickly you can review and give feedback.
Do I own the code afterwards?
Yes — all of it. Source repositories, design files, store accounts and infrastructure credentials transfer to you at handover. Nothing is held hostage.
I already have a developer. Can you still help?
Frequently. Common asks are unblocking a stalled release, adding an AI feature your team has not built before, or packaging a guide your product should have shipped with.
What happens after launch?
Thirty days of fixes are included. After that you can continue on a light monthly plan for updates, OS compatibility and new features — or take it in-house, your call.
